How it works

IoT sensors send each machine’s telemetry, new or old, to Mestti in real time, and AI helps read that data. That is how Industry 4.0 reaches the factory floor.

  1. Register the part once.

    Product, components and routing: which machine runs each step, cycle time, parts per cycle and setup. This record sets each machine’s target, the schedule and the cost per product.

    • Routing per component
    • Cycle and setup per machine
    • Cost per product

  2. Schedule it and know the delivery date.

    The order joins the machine queue with start and end calculated from the routing and real capacity. If the delivery doesn’t close, the delay shows up in the schedule before production starts.

    • Queue per machine
    • Expected date per order
    • Delays before they happen

  3. The sensor counts. Nobody types.

    IoT sensor installed without touching the PLC. It counts every cycle and logs stops and micro-stops on its own; the telemetry reaches Mestti in 4 seconds. Where there is no sensor, such as benches and assembly, the operator logs it on the tablet: start, pause, finish and quantity.

    • IoT sensor: count per cycle
    • Manual logging on the tablet
    • 4 s from shop floor to screen

  4. Inspection with proof.

    The inspector fills in the checklist on the phone, with measurement, photo and name. A measurement out of tolerance becomes non-conforming. Everything is kept for audits and customer answers.

    • Checklist per machine and product
    • Measurement, photo and owner
    • History for audits

    Questions before getting started

    Try with no commitmentAsk on WhatsApp
    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the number of machines and what you will use. The trial week is free and the sensor is on loan. At the end you get a proposal with your numbers. If you don’t continue, we remove the sensors, at no cost and with no commitment.

    What is Mestti?

    A Brazilian production management system for manufacturers of every size. Its own sensors measure every machine, and engineering, planning, the shop floor and quality use the same data.

    How long does it take to get started?

    Sensors ready within 7 days, installation in 1 day and a 1-week free trial before you decide.

    Will my operators feel watched?

    No. The sensor counts parts and sees whether the machine is running or stopped. It doesn’t watch people. Whoever was at the machine gives the reason for the stop, under their own name.

    I already have an ERP. Do I need to replace it?

    No. Mestti connects to your ERP: orders come from it and production goes back to it. The ERP keeps handling orders, purchasing and invoicing.

    Does it work on old machines?

    Yes. The sensor reads the count or the machine’s electrical signal. No PLC needed and no changes to the controls.

    Does it work without a sensor?

    Yes. On benches, assembly or machines without a sensor, the operator logs on the tablet: start, pause, finish and quantity. The data goes into the same target, OEE and queue as the machines with sensors.

    What if the factory’s internet goes down?

    The sensor stores everything and sends it when the connection returns. Nothing is lost.

    Is my data safe?

    Each company’s data is isolated and access is by permission. The original record cannot be changed. Otto only acts after a person approves.
