With the cycle time and parts per cycle, the hourly target is simple math, the same for everyone. When a machine falls short, the question stops being “who got it wrong?” and becomes “what happened?”.

What changes when engineering lives in the system.

The factory doesn’t depend on one person

Vacations, shift changes or an experienced person leaving don’t erase the right way to do it.

Delivery dates based on reality

Planners add up times that really exist, per machine, instead of estimating how long an order takes.

Pricing with real time

Costing uses standard time; production shows real time. The gap shows up before it becomes a loss.

What people ask about product engineering.

Is Mestti a CAD or PLM tool?

No. Mestti doesn’t design parts. It records the manufacturing structure: the components, operations, machines and times the shop floor and planners use every day.

What are parts per cycle?

It’s how many parts come out each time the machine completes a cycle. With a 4-cavity mold, for example, one cycle produces 4 parts. Mestti uses it to calculate targets and operation durations.

Can I register alternative machines?

Yes. Each operation can have a main machine and alternatives, so planners can redistribute the queue when a machine is down or overloaded.
