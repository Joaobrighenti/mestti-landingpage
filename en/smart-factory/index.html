Smart factory: what a smart factory looks like in practice

By João Brighenti, founder of Mestti · Reviewed on 2026-11-09

Short answer

A smart factory is a plant where machines, people and systems share data in real time and decisions come from that data. In practice it starts with three things: knowing each machine’s state right now, recording production and stops without paper and having someone watching the numbers to propose the next step.

Sensor → data → decisionWith the machines the factory already has.

No new machines and no long project.

What a smart factory has

  1. Connected machines

    IoT sensors on existing machines, no PLC and no changes to the controls.

  2. Real-time data

    State, counts and stops reach the system within seconds.

  3. One system

    Production, planning, quality and OEE on one base.

  4. AI that proposes

    An AI agent reads the data and suggests actions. A person approves.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need new machines for a smart factory?

No. Most machines, including old ones, can be measured with an external sensor, without a PLC.

Is a smart factory only for large plants?

No. Small plants feel every lost hour more and start with one department and a few machines.

Where does AI come in?

Mestti is an AI-powered platform. Otto, the AI agent, reads production data, explains deviations and proposes actions that only happen with a person’s approval.

Do I need an IT team?

Not to get started. The system runs in the cloud, opens in the browser and the app runs on a tablet. The network needs 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi near the machines.
