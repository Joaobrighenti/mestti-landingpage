What it does

An analyst who already knows your production.

  1. 01

    Interprets

    Reads stops, OEE, orders and quality and explains what changed and why.

  2. 02

    Converses

    Understands questions in natural language, from supervisor to director.

  3. 03

    Queries the system

    Pulls production, scheduling and quality from Mestti’s tools before answering.

  4. 04

    Keeps context

    Remembers what was said and picks the analysis up where it stopped.

How Otto acts

Every action goes through a person.

  1. ProposesDescribes the action and why.
  2. SimulatesShows the effect before anything changes.
  3. You approveAn authorized person decides.
  4. RunsMestti applies it under the system’s rules.
  5. ChecksChecks the result is the expected one.
  6. LogsIt stays in the history, with who approved.
  • The numbers come from the system.Indicators and calculations are Mestti’s own. Otto explains and organizes them.
  • Everyone sees what they could already see.Otto uses the user’s permissions, and each company’s data stays separate.
  • The machine’s record never changes.What the sensor recorded stays as it was recorded.

What people ask about Otto.

How do I open Otto?

From the Otto button at the top of any Mestti screen or with Ctrl + J. It opens beside the screen and can go full screen.

Does Otto replace my manager’s experience?

No. It shortens the path to the information and suggests where to look. The decision stays with whoever knows the factory.

Can Otto change production data?

Only actions Mestti supports, and always after an authorized person approves. The machine’s original record is never changed.

What language can I ask in?

In Portuguese, the way you’d talk to someone at the factory.
