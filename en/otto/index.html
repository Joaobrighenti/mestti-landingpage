Home / Otto

Ask your factory. The AI proposes; you decide.

Otto reads the Mestti data your user is allowed to see, investigates deviations and prepares actions. Before changing anything, it shows what it will do, simulates the effect and waits for your approval.

See an example

Everyday questions, answered with your data.

Production

“Where did I lose the most time this week?”

Stops by department, machine and reason, with the times they repeat.

Planning

“Which orders are at risk of being late?”

Scheduled orders that finish after the due date and what can be swapped in the queue.

Quality

“What is pending today?”

Inspections not done, non-conformities awaiting verification and who is responsible.

Six steps, and the third is always yours.

  1. ProposeOtto describes the action and why.
  2. SimulateShows the effect before changing anything.
  3. ApproveAn authorized person decides.
  4. ExecuteMestti applies the action through the system’s rules.
  5. VerifyChecks the result is what was expected.
  6. LogEverything is logged, with who approved it.

What Otto doesn’t do.

It doesn’t make up numbers

Calculations and indicators come from the system. Otto explains and organizes; it doesn’t create a parallel metric.

It can’t see what you can’t

It respects user permissions, and each company’s data stays separate.

It doesn’t act alone

No change happens without approval from an authorized person.

Technology and data

What people ask about Otto.

Does Otto replace my manager’s experience?

No. It shortens the way to the information and suggests where to look. The decision stays with whoever knows the factory.

What language can I ask in?

In Portuguese, the way you would talk to someone on the shop floor.

Can Otto change production data?

Only actions Mestti supports, and always after approval from an authorized person. The machine’s original record is never changed.
