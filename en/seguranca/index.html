A number nobody can fudge. Not even the system.

Fair accountability and good decisions depend on reliable data. That’s why Mestti builds its own technology, from sensor to AI, and treats every factory record as evidence.

Every layer is built by Mestti.

AI

Otto

An assistant that queries, investigates and proposes actions, always with human approval.

Meet Otto

Six commitments built into the code.

The original record never changes

What the sensor sent is stored exactly as it arrived. Operations work on an organized copy, and every human adjustment keeps its author and time.

The past isn’t rewritten

Corrections apply from now on. Changing a setting today doesn’t change yesterday’s numbers.

Each company isolated

Each company’s data is kept separate, and each person only sees what their access level allows.

The AI asks permission

Otto never writes directly to the factory’s data. It proposes, simulates and only acts after approval.

No internet, no loss

When the connection drops, the sensor stores events and sends them later. The period is rebuilt without inventing data.

“No signal” isn’t “stopped”

A communication failure shows up as a communication failure. It doesn’t become a stop or penalize the operator.

What people ask about data and security.

Read the Terms of use

Where is the data stored?

On Mestti’s cloud platform, with each company’s data kept separate. Contract details are in the Terms of use.

Who can change a production record?

Whoever has permission. Every change keeps who made it and when, and the sensor’s original record is kept.

Does Mestti use my data for another company?

No. One company’s data never appears to another, and Otto only reads the data of the company and user who asked.
