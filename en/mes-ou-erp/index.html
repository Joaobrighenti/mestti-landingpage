MES or ERP? In most factories, both.

The ERP answers “what did I sell, buy and invoice?”. The MES answers “what is happening in production right now, and why?”. One doesn’t replace the other: the ERP runs the business; the MES runs the shop floor.

Side by side.

Questions about MES and ERP.

Does Mestti replace my ERP?

No. Mestti handles product engineering, planning, execution and quality. Sales, purchasing, finance and invoicing stay in your ERP.

My ERP already has a production module. Do I need an MES?

If the module depends on someone typing in what happened, it shows the past and only what was written down. An MES with sensors shows the present, including the losses nobody writes down.
